CoverageGuard IQ

CoverageGuard IQ helps FQHCs identify patients at risk of Medicaid coverage loss, prioritize intervention, and manage redetermination follow-through through a human-reviewed, auditable workflow.

The Problem

On January 1, 2027, the retroactive eligibility window narrows from 90 days to 30 days for expansion enrollees and 60 days for everyone else. Every timeline for coverage recovery compresses with it.

Two clocks on one panel — and your staff has to know which patient is on which.

Eligible patients already fall off at redetermination: fragmented eligibility data, stale contacts, inconsistent follow-up, unclear ownership. Batch-cycle approaches will not fit inside the new window.

Retroactive coverage limits per the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (P.L. 119-21), effective for applications submitted on or after January 1, 2027.

Revenue Impact

Services delivered while a patient is uncovered depend on retroactive coverage to be reimbursed. As that window shortens, more of them fall outside it.

Patient Impact

Gaps interrupt care continuity, cut pharmacy access, and push patients to the ED. Eligible-but-uncovered patients absorb the most harm.

Intended Users
FQHC Executives Revenue Cycle Leaders Population Health Teams Eligibility Teams Care Management Pharmacy Leaders Health Plan Partners
Key Capabilities
  • Coverage reconciliation — merges EHR, payer 271, pharmacy claims, and contact data into a single confidence-scored view
  • Risk-stratified outreach — prioritizes patients by coverage risk, clinical urgency, and recertification timeline
  • Authorized representative workflow — supports submission on behalf of patients where authorized
  • Contact integrity — validates phone numbers and addresses before outreach attempts
  • Determination tracking — follows each case from identification through resolution
  • Audit trail — documents every outreach attempt, intervention, and outcome for compliance
  • Human oversight — all coverage determinations and submissions reviewed by staff
Current Maturity

Pilot-Ready: Available for controlled organizational testing with defined scope, safeguards, support, and success measures. CoverageGuard IQ uses synthetic demonstration data in its current interactive prototype. Production deployment requires partner-specific configuration, data agreements, and workflow validation.

Human Oversight

CoverageGuard IQ does not make autonomous coverage determinations. All eligibility assessments, outreach decisions, and submissions are reviewed and approved by authorized staff. The AI provides analysis, prioritization, and recommendations — the human decides.
