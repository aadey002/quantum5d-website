Dr. Adetoro Oriaifo

Quantum5D.ai was created by Dr. Adetoro Oriaifo after years leading pharmacy operations, governance initiatives, regulatory compliance, digital transformation, and revenue optimization within Federally Qualified Health Centers. Every application reflects operational challenges experienced firsthand — not hypothetical use cases.

Expertise spans 340B program management, specialty pharmacy, FQHC operations, payer partnerships, Medicaid strategy, governance, and AI-enabled workflow design — serving organizations nationwide.

Dr. Oriaifo holds a Doctor of Pharmacy from the University of Maryland School of Pharmacy and an MBA with a focus on healthcare administration. She is a Fellow of the American College of Healthcare Executives (FACHE), a 340B ACE (Apexus Certified Expert), and a Certified Health Center Executive in Finance (CHCEF).

Why Quantum5D.ai exists

Health centers run on spreadsheets, disconnected systems, and institutional memory. When the person who “knows how it works” leaves, the process breaks. When HRSA arrives for an OSV, the scramble begins. When Medicaid redeterminations accelerate, eligible patients fall off coverage because no one is watching.

Quantum5D.ai exists to change that. It is a purpose-built AI operating platform for Federally Qualified Health Centers — one platform, 15 applications, designed exclusively for the operational realities of safety-net healthcare.

Every application was built from a real operational challenge. No hypothetical use cases. No generic AI. Every workflow reflects how health centers actually operate — and every consequential action requires human review and approval.

What we stand for

Built from practice, not theory

Every application started as a real problem inside an FQHC. The workflows, the edge cases, the compliance requirements — all drawn from direct experience.

Human oversight, always
