## 1. Understanding Specialty Pharmacy Accreditation

### What is Specialty Pharmacy Accreditation?

Specialty pharmacy accreditation is a formal recognition that a pharmacy meets established standards for:

- **Quality patient care**
- **Safety protocols**
- **Operational excellence**
- **Regulatory compliance**
- **Continuous improvement**

### Benefits of Accreditation

**Clinical Benefits**
- Enhanced patient safety
- Improved clinical outcomes
- Standardized care protocols
- Evidence-based practices

**Business Benefits**
- Competitive differentiation
- Payer contract advantages
- Reduced liability exposure
- Operational efficiency

**Regulatory Benefits**
- Compliance assurance
- Audit readiness
- Risk mitigation
- Quality demonstration

## 2. Accreditation Bodies and Standards

### Major Accreditation Organizations

**ACHC (Accreditation Commission for Health Care)**
- Focus: Comprehensive specialty pharmacy services
- Standards: ACHC Specialty Pharmacy Standards
- Timeline: 6-12 months implementation

**URAC (Utilization Review Accreditation Commission)**
- Focus: Quality management and patient safety
- Standards: URAC Specialty Pharmacy Standards
- Timeline: 9-15 months implementation

**PCAB (Pharmacy Compounding Accreditation Board)**
- Focus: Compounding and sterile preparation
- Standards: PCAB Accreditation Standards
- Timeline: 6-9 months implementation

### Choosing the Right Accreditation

**Assessment Criteria**
1. **Service offerings alignment**
2. **Payer requirements**
3. **Market expectations**
4. **Implementation timeline**
5. **Cost considerations**
