2025 340B Program Changes: What Healthcare Organizations Need to Know Author: Dr. George Oriaifo, PharmD Organization: Quantum 5D Consulting Publication Date: January 2025 Reading Time: 15 minutes

Executive Summary The 340B Drug Pricing Program continues to evolve in 2025, with significant regulatory changes affecting covered entities, contract pharmacies, and program oversight. This comprehensive guide outlines the key changes healthcare organizations must understand to maintain compliance and optimize program benefits.

Table of Contents 1. Overview of 2025 340B Program Changes 2. Contract Pharmacy Regulations Update 3. Compliance and Oversight Enhancements 4. Impact on Covered Entities 5. Implementation Timeline and Action Items 6. Best Practices for Adaptation

1. Overview of 2025 340B Program Changes

Key Regulatory Updates - Enhanced manufacturer reporting requirements - Stricter contract pharmacy oversight - Updated patient eligibility criteria - New audit and compliance protocols

Impact on Program Participants The 2025 changes affect all 340B stakeholders: - Covered Entities: Enhanced documentation requirements - Contract Pharmacies: Stricter operational standards - Manufacturers: Expanded reporting obligations - Patients: Improved program transparency

2. Contract Pharmacy Regulations Update

New Registration Requirements 1. Enhanced Due Diligence - Comprehensive background checks - Financial stability verification - Operational capacity assessment 2. Ongoing Monitoring - Quarterly compliance reports - Real-time inventory tracking - Patient eligibility verification

Written Agreement Modifications Updated contract pharmacy agreements must include: - Data sharing protocols - Audit cooperation clauses - Duplicate discount prevention measures - Patient eligibility verification procedures

3. Compliance and Oversight Enhancements

HRSA Enforcement Activities Increased Audit Frequency - Annual compliance reviews for high-volume entities - Risk-based audit selection criteria - Enhanced follow-up procedures New Penalty Structure - Graduated sanctions for violations - Immediate suspension for serious infractions - Enhanced recoupment procedures

Technology Requirements Electronic Health Records Integration - Patient eligibility verification systems - Automated duplicate discount prevention - Real-time reporting capabilities
